Jagadish Rongali

Semiconductor Technical Leader | Verification & Emulation Architect
Senior IEEE Member DAC Engineering Track TPC Member IEEE Transactions Peer Reviewer Inventor Conference Speaker STEM Site Lead
Cedar Park, Texas, USA

I am a Semiconductor Technical Leader specializing in IP, subsystem, and SoC verification across automotive, wireless, mixed-signal, memory, graphics, and edge-computing domains.

My expertise spans verification architecture, UVM methodology, hardware-assisted verification, emulation, FPGA prototyping, ISO 26262 functional safety, silicon bring-up, and post-silicon debug. I lead verification initiatives focused on delivering high-quality silicon while driving methodology innovation and verification signoff.

Beyond product development, I actively contribute to the engineering community as a Senior IEEE Member, DAC Engineering Track Technical Program Committee Member, IEEE Transactions Peer Reviewer, conference speaker, inventor, mentor, and STEM Site Lead.

Through SiliconHive, I share practical insights on SystemVerilog, UVM, hardware emulation, DMA architectures, functional safety, AI acceleration, SoC design, and verification technologies.
